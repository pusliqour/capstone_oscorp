Notes

Two ways in

Retailers use the public Supabase API, and row-level security limits each one to their own store. Staff and developers use separate service-role access that retailers never see.

ML runs as jobs

A row in ml_triggers starts a job. The watcher runs the prediction, and the publisher writes the results back for the dashboard to read.

Research is kept apart

The research schema and Research Lab API are separate from the storefront data, so experiments never change what retailers see.

Not drawn

Install, verify and test scripts are left out to keep the diagram readable.
