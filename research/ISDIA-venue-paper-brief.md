## Important official-site inconsistency

The current homepage identifies **Sofitel Dubai Jumeirah Beach** as the venue in its hero block, while lower homepage copy says the event is hosted at the **UOWD campus**. The CFP only says Day 1 is onsite in Dubai. Do not print a physical venue in the manuscript or book travel until the organizers clarify it.

The homepage also mixes older text referring to ISDIA-2026 with the 2027 event. Use the 2027 CMT instance and current submission/deadline pages, but confirm the final date, venue, proceedings volume, and registration terms by email before paying.

## Recommended positioning for Jiran

**Primary track:** trustworthy AI-enabled information-system design for operational resource allocation.
**Domain continuity:** inventory optimization and inter-retailer redistribution remain the demonstrated graduation-project domain.
**Generalized claim:** the architecture can represent distributed resources beyond groceries, but this is an architectural generalization until evaluated in another domain.
**Current contribution:** recoverable architecture, source-neutral ingestion, explicit AI abstention/null semantics, data-governance boundary, and a reproducible evaluation protocol.
**Do not claim yet:** model accuracy, savings, waste reduction, adoption, live Square integration, recovered historical data, or validated generalization.
