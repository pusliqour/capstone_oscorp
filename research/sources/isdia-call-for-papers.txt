Call For Papers

ISDIA-2027 invites high-quality, original research submissions from scholars, practitioners, and students in the areas of Information System Design and Intelligent Applications. The conference will be held in hybrid mode on 9th - 10th, January 2027, with Day 1 hosted onsite in Dubai and Day 2 conducted virtually via Webex. We welcome contributions across theoretical and applied dimensions of intelligent systems, with a focus on interdisciplinary innovation, real-world impact, and responsible AI.
Building on the success of ISDIA 2025, which focused on “AI for Healthcare”, ISDIA-2027 expands the conversation toward Agentic AI and Sustainability. Accepted and registered papers will be published in Springer Nature's Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, indexed in SCOPUS, DBLP, and Web of Science, subject to Springer’s editorial guidelines.
