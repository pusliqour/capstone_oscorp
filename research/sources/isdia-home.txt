About ISDIA-2027

The International Conference on Information System Design and Intelligent Applications (ISDIA), formerly known as INDIA, has a long-standing legacy of bringing together global researchers, academics, and industry professionals. Since its inception in 2012, ISDIA has grown into a prestigious platform recognized for fostering interdisciplinary dialogue and showcasing innovations in intelligent systems.
Over the past decade, it has been hosted in India, Vietnam, Mauritius, and the UAE, attracting participation from dozens of countries and hundreds of delegates. With successful partnerships, including publication through Springer Nature, ISDIA continues to push the boundaries of academic excellence and applied research in intelligent systems.

Conference Theme — Trustworthy AI - Governance, Risk and Responsible Innovation

As Artificial Intelligence transitions from experimental technology to mission-critical enterprise infrastructure, the central question is no longer can AI do it — but should it, and can we trust it to? The 11th edition of ISDIA, in collaboration with UOWD Dubai and Protiviti, takes on this defining challenge of our era. Trustworthy AI encompasses the full spectrum of concerns that accompany intelligent systems at scale: algorithmic transparency, explainability, fairness, data privacy, regulatory compliance, and ethical accountability.
ISDIA-2027 invites researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and industry leaders to explore how trust can be engineered into AI from the ground up — spanning model design, deployment pipelines, organizational governance, and societal impact.

About University of Wollongong in Dubai (UOWD)

ISDIA-2027 is proudly hosted by the University of Wollongong in Dubai (UOWD), a pioneer in international higher education in the UAE. Located in Dubai Knowledge Park, UOWD provides an ideal environment for global collaboration with strong regional and international networks.
UOW is ranked among the top 200 universities globally with a 5+ star QS ranking. Established in 1993, UOWD is the first international Australian university in the UAE — home to 3,500+ students from 108 nationalities. All degrees are accredited by CAA and licensed by KHDA, recognised by AACSB, ACCA, ACS, Engineers Australia, and others.
The School of Computer Science offers programmes in Computer Science, Cyber Security, Game & Mobile Development, AI and Big Data, IT Management, Digital Transformation, and PhD through UOW Australia.
